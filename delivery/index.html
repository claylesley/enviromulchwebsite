Delivery & Pickup

Load it yourself at the yard or have our trucks bring it to you. Delivery throughout the Upstate.

Yard Pickup

Bring a pickup truck or trailer and we’ll load you with the loader. Tarp your load for the drive home.

  • Loaded by the scoop with our loader
  • Bring a tarp and straps
  • Know your truck or trailer capacity

Pickup location: 1270 Chastain Rd., Liberty, SC 29657, at the Hwy. 123 and Road 27 exit.

Yard hours: Mon–Fri 8:00–5:30, Sat 8:00–12:00. Call first if rainy. A scoop is the same as a yard of material; 1 scoop at 2″ thick covers about 150 sq ft.

Delivery

Our dump trucks deliver bulk material to homes, job sites, schools and businesses. We dump where the truck can safely reach.

  • Tell us the exact drop spot and any gate or overhead wires
  • Keep the drop area clear of cars and soft ground
  • Mixed loads: ask when ordering

Delivery area: Greenville, Spartanburg, Anderson, Oconee, Pickens, Easley, Powdersville, Central, Six Mile, Clemson and Pendleton, and other areas in the Upstate of South Carolina.

Blower Truck Installation AvailableFor large installs, our blower trucks place mulch and playground surfacing quickly and evenly, right where you need it. Call 864-843-2448 for a quote.

Good to Know

  • First dump included, $10.00 per each additional dump.
  • Truck spread fee on gravel deliveries $20 above delivery fee.
  • Bales delivered $1.05/bale + fuel surcharge.
  • Gated entrances or rough terrain may add fees.
  • Fuel/supply chain surcharge may apply when fuel prices are elevated.
  • Thursday–Saturday deliveries may need several days’ notice.
  • Card payments: an invoice is sent and must be paid before the order is added to the delivery schedule.
  • Prices may change without notice.
