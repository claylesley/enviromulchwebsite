Overview

A landscape materials yard in Liberty, SC and a division of A. King Forest Products.

Nobody Beats Our Prices

Rooted in Wood

EnviroMulch is the landscape materials division of A. King Forest Products in Liberty, South Carolina, alongside King Pallet Co. Working with wood every day means we understand mulch from the source.

We carry all types of mulch plus colored mulch, play chips, horse hay and bedding, soil, sand, pine and wheat straw, stone and palletized stone, decorative stone and landscaping products, with delivery throughout the Upstate. Cards accepted.
